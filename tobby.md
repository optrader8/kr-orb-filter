## 📊 Core Concepts

### 1. **Opening Range Breakout (ORB)**

* **Definition**: Trading strategy based on breakouts from the opening range (first X minutes of trading).
* **Time Windows**: Commonly 5, 15, or 30 minutes after market open.
* **Breakout Triggers**:
  * **Long Signal**: Price clears the ORB high with volume confirmation.
  * **Short Signal**: Price breaks below the ORB low with volume confirmation.
* **Rationale**: The opening captures overnight information, liquidity positioning, and directional bias for the entire session.
* **Enhancement**: Combine with gap analysis and volume filters for higher probability setups.

### 2. **Short-Term Price Patterns**

* **Pattern Analysis**: Study relationships between open, high, low, and close across multiple timeframes.
* **Key Filters**:
  * **Move-off-Open (MOO)**: Direction and magnitude of initial market movement.
  * **Gap Magnitude**: Size and type of overnight gaps (up, down, or inside).
  * **Day Structure**: Inside days, outside days, and narrow range patterns.
* **Application**: Patterns confirm whether an ORB signal reflects genuine momentum or false breakout.

### 3. **Volatility Contraction → Expansion Cycle**

* **Market Principle**: Markets cycle between periods of low volatility (contraction) and high volatility (expansion).
* **Trading Edge**: Anticipate volatility expansion following periods of extreme contraction.
* **Implementation**:
  * Monitor Average True Range (ATR) compressions.
  * Identify narrow range patterns as volatility setup indicators.
  * Position for breakouts when volatility is coiled and ready to expand.

### 4. **NR7 Pattern (Narrow Range 7)**

* **Definition**: Current day's range is the narrowest of the previous seven trading sessions.
* **Calculation**:
  ```
  Daily Range = High - Low
  NR7 = Current Range < Min(Previous 6 Days' Ranges)
  ```
* **Trading Application**:
  * Tag NR7 sessions as potential breakout candidates.
  * Monitor next session for breakout through NR7 boundaries.
  * Combine with ORB signals and volume expansion for confirmation.
* **Logic**: Extreme range compression often precedes significant directional moves.

### 5. **Bias & Context Filters**

* **Environmental Analysis**: Never trade signals in isolation; always consider broader market context.
* **Filter Categories**:
  * **Index Trend**: Overall market direction (SPY, QQQ for US; KOSPI for Korea).
  * **Sector Momentum**: Industry group strength or weakness.
  * **Macro Catalysts**: Economic releases, earnings, geopolitical events.
  * **Liquidity Screens**: Volume and bid-ask spread requirements.
* **Purpose**: Improves signal selectivity and reduces false positive breakouts.

## ⚖️ Risk Management Framework

### 1. **Stop Loss Rules**

* **Primary Stop**: Opposite ORB boundary (if long, stop below ORB low).
* **Volatility Stop**: 1.5-2.0 × ATR from entry point.
* **Time Stop**: Close position if no favorable movement within predetermined timeframe.
* **Maximum Risk**: Never exceed 1-2% of account value per trade.

### 2. **Profit Targets**

* **Fixed Risk:Reward**: Common ratios of 1:2 or 1:3 (risk $1 to make $2-3).
* **Session Close**: Take profits at end of trading session to avoid overnight risk.
* **Pattern-Based**: Target previous support/resistance levels or measured moves.
* **Trailing Stops**: Move stops to breakeven after 1:1 reward achieved.

### 3. **Position Sizing**

* **Fixed-Fraction**: Consistent percentage of account per trade (1-2%).
* **Volatility-Adjusted**: Larger positions in low volatility, smaller in high volatility.
* **Drawdown Rules**: Reduce position size after losing streaks to preserve capital.
* **Maximum Exposure**: Limit total market exposure to prevent catastrophic loss.

## 🎯 Trade Workflow

### Pre-Market Preparation
1. **Universe Screening**: Identify instruments showing NR7 or multi-day narrow ranges.
2. **Gap Analysis**: Assess overnight gaps and their implications for opening range.
3. **Market Bias**: Determine overall market sentiment using index futures and global markets.
4. **Volume Expectations**: Review average volume patterns for target instruments.

### Opening Window Execution
1. **Range Recording**: Document high and low prices during chosen ORB period (5, 15, or 30 minutes).
2. **Pattern Recognition**: Confirm presence of setup patterns (NR7, inside days, etc.).
3. **Volume Monitoring**: Ensure breakout occurs with above-average volume participation.
4. **Signal Validation**: Verify breakout aligns with broader market bias before execution.

### Position Management
1. **Entry Execution**: Enter immediately upon confirmed breakout through ORB boundary.
2. **Stop Placement**: Set protective stops according to predetermined risk rules.
3. **Target Monitoring**: Track progress toward profit objectives throughout session.
4. **Exit Discipline**: Close positions according to plan without emotional override.

### Post-Market Review
1. **Trade Documentation**: Log entry/exit prices, reasoning, and outcome in trading journal.
2. **Pattern Analysis**: Review successful and failed setups for future improvement.
3. **Performance Metrics**: Update win rate, average win/loss, and risk-adjusted returns.
4. **Strategy Refinement**: Adjust parameters based on recent market behavior patterns.

## 📈 Advanced Pattern Recognition

### 1. **Inside Day + NR7 Combination**
* **Setup**: Inside day that is also NR7 (double compression signal).
* **Entry**: Breakout of inside day boundaries in direction of broader trend.
* **Logic**: Multiple compression signals increase breakout probability.

### 2. **Gap + ORB Strategy**
* **Small Gaps**: Gaps < 1% often fill then continue in gap direction.
* **Large Gaps**: Gaps > 2% may reverse after initial continuation.
* **Integration**: Use gap size to modify ORB breakout expectations.

### 3. **Multiple Timeframe Confirmation**
* **Daily Context**: Ensure intraday signals align with daily chart patterns.
* **Weekly Bias**: Consider longer-term trend when evaluating breakout direction.
* **Sector Alignment**: Verify individual stock signals match sector movement.
