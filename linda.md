## 📊 Core Principles

### 1. **The Holy Grail Setup (HG)**

* **Definition**: A pullback in a strong trend that creates a high-probability continuation entry.
* **Requirements**:
  * **Strong trend** – ADX > 30 or moving averages clearly aligned.
  * **Pullback** – 3-5 bars retracement to key support/resistance.
  * **Momentum divergence** – RSI or Stochastic shows oversold/overbought.
* **Entry**: Break of pullback high/low in direction of main trend.
* **Logic**: Markets trend, but rarely in straight lines. Pullbacks offer better risk/reward entries.

### 2. **ADX/DMI System**

* **ADX (Average Directional Index)**: Measures trend strength.
* **Trading Rules**:
  * **ADX > 30**: Strong trend – use trend-following strategies.
  * **ADX < 20**: Choppy market – avoid breakouts, use mean reversion.
  * **+DI > -DI**: Bullish bias (long setups preferred).
  * **-DI > +DI**: Bearish bias (short setups preferred).
* **Application**: Primary filter for all trading decisions.

### 3. **Turtle Soup Strategy**

* **Concept**: Fade false breakouts from 20-day highs/lows.
* **Setup**:
  * Price breaks above 20-day high but closes below it (failed breakout).
  * Next day, short if price trades below previous day's low.
  * Reverse for longs: break below 20-day low, fail, then rally.
* **Risk Management**: Stop above/below the failed breakout level.
* **Logic**: Breakout failures often lead to strong moves in opposite direction.

### 4. **Anti-Swing Setup**

* **Pattern**: Counter-trend move after 2-3 days of strong directional movement.
* **Entry Criteria**:
  * 2-day RSI < 10 (for longs) or RSI > 90 (for shorts).
  * Stochastic %K crosses above %D (longs) or below %D (shorts).
  * Enter on break of prior day's high/low.
* **Target**: Return to 5-period moving average.
* **Best For**: Short-term mean reversion in trending markets.

### 5. **Opening Range Breakout (ORB) - Linda's Version**

* **Time Frame**: First 30 minutes of trading session.
* **Enhancement**: Combine with volume and gap analysis.
* **Filters**:
  * **Volume**: Must be above 20-day average volume.
  * **Gap**: Prefer small gaps (< 1% for stocks) for continuation.
  * **Market Bias**: Align with overall index direction.
* **Risk**: 1.5 × opening range or previous day's low/high.

### 6. **Moving Average Strategies**

* **20/50 EMA Crossover**:
  * Long when 20 EMA crosses above 50 EMA with volume expansion.
  * Short when 20 EMA crosses below 50 EMA.
* **3-Period Moving Average**:
  * Very short-term entries: Buy above 3-period MA in uptrend.
  * Tight stops: 2-3 points below 3-period MA.
* **Application**: Different timeframes for different holding periods.

## ⚖️ Risk Management Framework

### 1. **Position Sizing Rules**
* **2% Rule**: Never risk more than 2% of account on single trade.
* **Heat**: Total portfolio heat (open risk) never exceeds 6-8%.
* **Correlation**: Reduce position size when trading correlated instruments.

### 2. **Stop Loss Methodology**
* **Technical Stops**: Below support, above resistance, or beyond pattern.
* **Volatility Stops**: 1.5-2 × Average True Range (ATR).
* **Time Stops**: Close position if no movement within expected timeframe.
* **Money Management Stop**: Maximum 2% loss per trade.

### 3. **Profit Taking Strategy**
* **Scale Out**: Take 1/3 at 1:1, 1/3 at 2:1, trail final 1/3.
* **Target Zones**: Previous highs/lows, round numbers, measured moves.
* **Trailing Stops**: Move stop to breakeven after 1:1, then trail by ATR.

## 🎯 Trade Management Workflow

### Pre-Market Preparation
1. **Market Assessment**: Check overnight news, futures, foreign markets.
2. **Scan Setup**: Identify stocks meeting criteria (ADX, volume, patterns).
3. **Bias Determination**: Overall market direction using index analysis.

### Trading Session
1. **Opening Analysis**: Monitor gaps, volume, and opening range formation.
2. **Pattern Recognition**: Look for HG, Turtle Soup, Anti-Swing setups.
3. **Execution**: Enter with predetermined risk and target levels.
4. **Management**: Monitor position according to plan, no emotion-based changes.

### Post-Market Review
1. **Trade Journal**: Record entry reason, exit reason, lessons learned.
2. **Performance Analysis**: Weekly/monthly P&L and statistics review.
3. **Strategy Adjustment**: Modify parameters based on market conditions.

## 📈 High-Probability Setups

### 1. **The Volatility Breakout**
* **Identification**: 5-day Average True Range expanding after contraction.
* **Entry**: Break of narrow range high/low with volume confirmation.
* **Target**: 2-3 × the narrow range measurement.

### 2. **Gap Fade Strategy**
* **Setup**: Large gap (>2%) in direction opposite to overall trend.
* **Entry**: When price fills 50% of gap, enter in fade direction.
* **Risk**: Beyond gap extreme.
* **Best For**: Over-reactions to news in strong trending stocks.

### 3. **Momentum Continuation**
* **Pattern**: Strong move (>5%) followed by tight consolidation (2-3 days).
* **Entry**: Break of consolidation in direction of initial move.
* **Volume**: Must confirm with expansion on breakout.
